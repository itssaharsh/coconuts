# Video plan: Coconuts (under 3:00, public on YouTube)

Emulator footage is allowed by the rules. Every shot below comes from the IWSDK desktop emulator (Quest 3
profile unless noted) through `tools/xr/record.mjs` (screencast frames) with the mix recorded in-page
(`?sound` keeps the audio graph on in an automation run; `window.__coconuts.start()/stop()` records it).
Voice-over is the Director's own TTS voice (`/api/tts`) plus one human line at the start if wanted.
