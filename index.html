Coconuts: the Serial

A silent-film serial, in mixed reality

You make every sound.

A toy theatre on your desk plays a silent-film serial, and nothing happens on stage until you make its sound. Then its thief jumps out of the picture onto your table, and an AI Director writes the next reel.

One question before the curtain. Between reels you can say what happens next, in your own words. May the Director hear you?

Your room dims, a theatre rises on your desk; tap the clapper.

How to play

  1. 1Tap the clapper

    The curtain rises on the first reel. In the headset, tap it with a finger; here, click it or press Space.

  2. 2Make the sound

    The stage waits for you. Coconut shells for hooves, a little door to knock and slam, a desk bell, a thunder sheet, a slide whistle and a bag of glass: three knocks open the castle gate, and the knight’s horse runs only as fast as you clop.

  3. 3Watch it break out

    Your thunder bursts the theatre open to the size of your wall. Then your crash of glass breaks the picture itself: the thief jumps down onto your desk, and your slide whistle sends him over the edge.

  4. 4Choose what happens next

    After each reel a card asks what happens next. Sound a prop to choose, or hold the megaphone and say your own idea, and the next reel is written around it.

In the headset every prop answers a pat, a pinch from afar, or a look and a pinch.

What happens next?

An AI Director writes each next reel around the sound you chose and what your sounds did before. When it is away, a written reel stands in, so the show goes on.

Loading the reel…

    Pinch the clapperboard and the film rolls.

    A silent film plays on a little screen. The props on your table make the sound: coconuts for the running feet, the tiny door for the bang, the slide whistle for the fall. Your take plays back with the film, and the Director gives you one line about it.

    • Three public-domain films, one to two minutes each.
    • Plays with gaze and a single pinch on Meta VR Glasses.
    • Your take becomes a link anyone can watch in a browser.

    How it works

    • WATCHthe cue card slides in

    • HITclap the coconuts

    • PLAY IT BACKthe film with your sound

    What's real: the films are original prints from the Internet Archive; every sound is synthesized on your headset; the Director's line comes from a model, with a written line when offline.
