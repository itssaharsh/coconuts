# Coconuts foley props

Generated by `assets-src/build_props.py`; do not edit by hand. After export every `public/models/*.glb` was
re-imported into an empty Blender scene and measured; triangles are cross-checked against the GLB index accessors.

**Axes.** glTF/three.js, metres, Y up. W = X extent, H = Y extent, D = Z extent. The exporter maps Blender
(x, y, z) to glTF (x, z, -y): **Blender -Y becomes glTF +Z** (the front of every prop faces a viewer on +Z,
like the three.js default camera), and **Blender +Y becomes glTF -Z** (glTF forward, away from that viewer).
Origins sit at the base centre (bottom of the bounding box) unless a note says otherwise.

## Moving parts and anchors (glTF coordinates)

- **door / Leaf**: node origin on the hinge axis at [-0.0345, 0.0075, 0.0045] (the leaf's front-left edge). The leaf mesh runs x 0.000..0.069 from it, so the hinge is on the viewer's left. `Leaf.rotation.y = -1.2` swings the free edge to (0.025, 0.000, 0.064), toward the viewer (+Z): **negative rotation.y opens the door toward the audience**, positive swings it away through the frame (no stop is modelled, both work). `Knob` is a child of `Leaf` at [0.0615, 0.0615, -0.0035], so it swings with it.
- **bell / Button**: origin at the bottom of the button at [0, 0.037, 0]; translate it -0.003 on Y to press it.
- **thunder_sheet / Sheet**: origin at the middle of the sheet's top edge [0.012, 0.153, 0]; rotate about X to swing it on its cords. `Handle` is a child of `Sheet` (local [0, -0.1205, 0], the dowel centre), so shaking the handle shakes the sheet.
- **slide_whistle / Slide**: origin on the barrel axis at the open end [0.05, 0.026, 0]; the slide moves along X (translate -X up to 0.020 to push it in, +X to pull it out).

The curtain's lowest point is 5 mm above the opening and the tails stay outside it. Footlights (L, R) origins on the ledge at [-0.26, 0.02, 0.048] and [0.26, 0.02, 0.048], cans tilted 40 degrees back toward the screen.
- **glass_bag / Shards**: material `Glass` is exported with alphaMode BLEND (alpha 0.6).
- Materials are Principled BSDF only, no textures or UVs; roughness 0.6 to 0.8 except `Steel` (0.35, metallic 0.6, as briefed) and `Glass` (0.3, so it reads as glass); `Brass` is metallic 0.6.
- Curved parts are smooth shaded with hard edges kept sharp. Every mesh is a closed solid exported single sided (`doubleSided: false`) except `Curtain`, whose open drape surfaces are exported double sided.
- Re-running the script rewrites identical bytes (deterministic: fixed seeds, no bmesh primitives with unstable face order).
